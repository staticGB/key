What do these words mean?

Note: one musical sound, like A or C#. Pitch: how high or low a note is.

Key: the small group of notes a song mostly sticks to, plus its home note. "A minor" means home is A and the mood is darker; "major" is brighter.

Scale: that group of notes lined up from low to high. Playing only those notes is why nothing sounds wrong.

Chord: several notes played together. The chords listed are the ones that naturally fit the key.

Your own sound: a bark or a squeak has its own pitch. The page finds that "natural note", then speeds it up or slows it down for each key, so every key lands on a note that fits the song. If it guessed wrong, fix the natural note yourself.

Tuning: if everything sounds slightly off, nudge the Tuning slider. Some songs are tuned a little sharp or flat.
